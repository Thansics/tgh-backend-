Terms & Privacy

Thansics Grandma's Honey & Broom (TGH)

1. Who we are

TGH is a small family business selling homemade honey and handwoven brooms, run by Grandma and family. This page explains what happens when you use our website: what we collect, why, and how your order and account information is handled.

2. Information we collect

  • When you place an order: your name, phone number, delivery address, and (if you give it) your email.
  • If you create an account: your name, phone number, email, address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your actual password — only a scrambled version that can't be reversed).
  • If you pay online: your payment is handled entirely by Paystack, a licensed Nigerian payment processor. We never see or store your card details — only whether the payment succeeded.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare, deliver, and keep you updated on your order (by email and/or WhatsApp).
  • To let you log in and see your past orders, if you create an account.
  • To verify it's really you — email verification codes and password reset codes exist to protect your account from anyone else trying to use your phone number or email.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with advertisers.

4. Services we rely on

To run smoothly, TGH uses a few trusted outside services, each only for the specific job listed:

  • Brevo — sends verification codes, password reset codes, and order emails.
  • Paystack — processes online payments securely.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Cloud API) — notifies Grandma when a new order comes in.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data passed to them.

5. How long we keep information

Order records and accounts are kept so we can track order history and handle any issues. If you'd like your account or data deleted, message us on WhatsApp and we'll remove it.

6. Cookies & local storage

When you log in, your browser stores a small login token (not a tracking cookie) so you stay signed in. It's only used to recognize your account on this site — it isn't used for advertising or tracking you elsewhere.

7. Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to see what information we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Just reach out — contact details are below.

8. Orders & payments

Placing an order is a request to buy — we'll confirm it and get it ready. Prices are listed in Naira (₦) and may change without notice. If you pay online via Paystack, that payment is final once confirmed; for delivery issues or order problems, message us directly and we'll make it right.

9. Contact us

Questions about this page, your data, or an order? Reach us on WhatsApp at 0816 803 7487, or by phone at 0707 126 7515.

Note: This page is written in plain language to be clear and honest about how TGH handles your information — it isn't a substitute for formal legal advice. If TGH grows into a larger operation, it's a good idea to have a lawyer review and formalize these terms.
