💛 The story

Meet the Grandma behind TGH

Thansics Grandma has been keeping bees and weaving brooms for as long as anyone in the family can remember. What started as jars shared with neighbors turned into TGH — honey and brooms made exactly the way she's always made them, just for a few more doorsteps now.

No factories, no fillers — just patience, bees, and good broom-corn.
